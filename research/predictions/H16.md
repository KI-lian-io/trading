# H16 prediction: Kronos forecast as a daily long/flat signal (written before testing)

Written: 2026-10-23, before any Kronos forecast was generated on our data.

## Source

Kronos, "A Foundation Model for the Language of Financial Markets" (Shi et al., arXiv 2508.02739, AAAI 2026),
github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos at commit 67b630e, model NeoQuasar/Kronos-small with Kronos-Tokenizer-base.

## Rules (fixed before testing)

- Pairs: BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT, Binance spot, 4h candles. Each pair has its own half of a 1000 USDT wallet.
- Period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-22. Training-data cutoff of the published weights is not stated; the
  paper is from August 2025, so 2026 data should be unseen by the model.
- Once a day at 00:00 UTC, using only candles that have closed: feed the last 400 4h candles
  (OHLCV + amount) and forecast the next 6 candles (24h). T=1.0, top_p=0.9, sample_count=5 (README
  defaults), fixed random seed.
- Signal: predicted 24h return = forecast close of candle 6 / last close - 1.
  Long for the next 24h if it is above +1% (about 2.5x the 0.4% round-trip cost at 2x fees), else flat.
  Staying long on consecutive days costs nothing extra.
- Long-only spot (no shorts). Fees: 0.2% per side (2x Binance spot). Also a 0.1% run for comparison.
- Baselines: buy-and-hold each pair; 1000 random-entry strategies with the same number of long days.

## Prediction

- Direction hit rate (sign of predicted vs actual 24h return, all days): 49-54%.
- Days long: 10-40% of days.
- Strategy result at 2x fees: between -15% and +10% over the period.
- It will not beat the 95th percentile of the random-entry strategies.

## Pass / fail

Pass only if, at 2x fees, the combined strategy is positive, beats buy-and-hold, and beats the 95th
percentile of the random-entry strategies. Anything else is a fail. No change of threshold, model size,
lookback or sampling after seeing the results.

## Result (added after the test, 2026-10-23)

590 forecasts (295 days x 2 pairs), 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-22, 1000 USDT split equally.

| Fees per side | Strategy | Buy-and-hold | Random entries, median | Random, 95th percentile | Beats random |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2% (main run) | -20.7% | -13.7% | -17.2% | +1.8% | 35% |
| 0.1% | -16.6% | -13.5% | -10.2% | +10.7% | 26% |

| Pair | Days long | Direction hit rate | Correlation forecast vs actual | Avg next-24h return when long | Avg all days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 43 (15%) | 51.5% | +0.10 | -0.10% | +0.00% |
| ETH | 56 (19%) | 49.8% | +0.09 | -0.26% | -0.02% |

Versus the prediction: hit rate and days long as predicted; return slightly worse than the predicted
range (-20.7% vs -15% to +10%); did not beat the random 95th percentile, as predicted. **Verdict: failed.**
The forecasts are close to a coin flip; the days the model was most bullish were followed by small losses.

Implementation note: `Kronos.from_pretrained` leaves the model in training mode; the script calls `eval()`
first (otherwise attention dropout is on, and on Apple MPS it crashes).
