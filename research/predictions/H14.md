# H14 prediction: delta-neutral funding-rate carry (written before testing)

Written: 2026-10-15, before any funding statistics or backtest of this strategy were computed.

## Source

He, Manela, Ross, von Wachter (2022), "Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures", arXiv 2212.06888: funding
arbitrage kept a high Sharpe after Binance's top fees, but the opportunity shrank over time.

## Rules (fixed before testing)

- Universe: the 12 USDT pairs (BTC ETH SOL XRP ADA DOGE LINK AVAX LTC DOT TRX ATOM), one equal sleeve each,
  1000 USDT total. Period 2023-01-01 to 2026-10-14 (Binance USDT-M funding history starts there in our data).
- Each sleeve: buy q coins on spot, short q on the USDT-M perpetual. Short leverage 2x (EU retail cap), so
  capital per sleeve = 1.5 x notional (1.0 spot + 0.5 margin).
- Funding: at every 8h print the short receives (or pays, if negative) q x mark price x funding rate.
- Prices: spot 1h close and perp 1h close for fills; mark close for funding. Basis changes count as P&L.
- Rebalance: if the perp price rises 40% above the last entry, close and reopen both legs (full fees), so the
  short is never near liquidation.
- Fees (main run): 2x Binance: spot 0.2% per side, perp 0.1% per side. Also a 1x run (0.1% / 0.05%).
- Version A, always on: enter at the start, hold to the end.
- Version B, switching: at each funding print, enter if the mean of the last 9 prints (3 days) is above
  0.01% per 8h; exit if it is below 0. Idle capital earns nothing.

## Prediction

- Version A, 2x fees: about 5-8% a year on capital in 2023-24, 2-4% a year in 2025-26,
  max drawdown below 5%. Total fees under 1% of capital a year.
- Version B: similar to A in 2023-24, lower or equal in 2025-26 because of switching costs.
- Far below buy-and-hold in 2023-24, far above it in 2025-26 (the market fell about 45%).

## Pass / fail

Pass only if, at 2x fees, the better version earns more than 5% a year over the full period, more than 3% a
year in 2025-26 (roughly a EUR cash yield), and has a max drawdown below 10%. Otherwise it is not worth the
exchange, tax and operational risk. No re-tuning of thresholds after seeing the results.

## Result (added after the test, 2026-10-15)

Return on capital, 1000 USDT, 12 pairs, 2023-01-01 to 2026-10-14.

| Version | Fees | Total | Per year 2023-26 | Per year 2023-24 | Per year 2025-26 | Max DD | Fees paid | Funding received |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A always on | 2x | +10.6% | 2.7% | 4.6% | 0.7% | 0.9% | 24 USDT | 131 USDT |
| A always on | 1x | +11.9% | 3.0% | 5.1% | 0.7% | 0.9% | 12 USDT | 132 USDT |
| B switching | 2x | +8.2% | 2.1% | 3.9% | 0.1% | 0.6% | 36 USDT | 115 USDT |
| B switching | 1x | +10.1% | 2.6% | 4.7% | 0.2% | 0.5% | 18 USDT | 116 USDT |

Average funding (annualized, on notional): BTC 9.9% in 2023-24 and 4.1% in 2025-26; ETH 10.6% / 3.6%.
TRX, ATOM, DOT and SOL were negative in 2025-26.

Versus the prediction: drawdown and fees as predicted; 2023-24 slightly below (4.6% vs 5-8%);
2025-26 far below (0.7% vs 2-4%); B below A as predicted. **Verdict: failed** the pass rule
(needed >5% a year overall and >3% a year in 2025-26). The hedge works; the carry is too small.
