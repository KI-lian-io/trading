# H13 prediction: Bitcoin multi-timeframe MACD trend (written before testing)

Written: 2026-10-15, before any backtest of this strategy was run.

## Source

Quantpedia, "How to Design a Simple Multi-Timeframe Trend Strategy on Bitcoin" (2025), listed in
paperswithbacktest/awesome-systematic-trading. https://quantpedia.com/how-to-design-a-simple-multi-timeframe-trend-strategy-on-bitcoin/

## Rules (as published, final "D1H1 + Stop" version)

- BTC, long-only, one position, whole wallet.
- Entry on 1h: MACD(12, 26, 9) line crosses above its signal line, AND the daily MACD(12, 26, 9) line is above its signal line (daily value only from the last closed daily candle).
- Exit: at the close of the first 1h candle that closes below its open.
- No other stop. Fees and slippage: not stated in the article.

Published results (Gemini BTC/USD, Dec 2018 to Nov 2025): Sharpe 1.07, Calmar 0.87 (D1H1 + Stop);
pure 1h MACD Sharpe 0.33, about 2,262 trades; buy-and-hold "over 60%" a year, max drawdown "nearly -80%".

## Test setup

- Binance BTC/USDT spot, 1h with 1d informative, 2019-01-01 to 2026-10-14.
- Fees: 0.2% per side (2x Binance spot 0.1%) as the main run; also 0.1% and 0% for comparison.
- No parameter changes. One run per fee level. Year breakdown.

## Prediction

- Trades: 150 to 300 per year (exit on the first red 1h candle makes holds short).
- 0% fees: roughly matches the article, Sharpe 0.8 to 1.1, max drawdown 20 to 35%.
- 0.1% fees: near break-even, CAGR 0 to 8%.
- 0.2% fees (main run): negative, CAGR -5 to -20%. Average gross gain per trade will be smaller than the 0.4% round-trip cost.
- Far below buy-and-hold return over the full period; smaller drawdown than buy-and-hold.

## Pass / fail

Pass only if, at 0.2% fees: CAGR above 10%, max drawdown below 30%, and positive in both 2019-2024 and 2025-2026.
Anything else is a fail. No re-tuning after seeing the results.
